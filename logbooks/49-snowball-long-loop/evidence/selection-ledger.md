## Metric definitions

- **Task vs attempt:** a *task* is one distinct evaluation task identity; an *attempt* is one scheduled rollout slot of that task (per the pinned Micro eval policy, AIME24 and OlympiadBench use 10 attempts per task, GPQA/TBLite/TB2 use 3, all others 1 — so 200 tasks expand to 440 final-eval attempts). In **RL validation** each checkpoint is scheduled over 120 validation attempts; the receipts record `num_attempted`, `num_scored` and `excluded_count`. **Fully scored validations** (exp2 steps 6–20; exp1 steps 14/16) are 120 attempted / 120 scored / 0 excluded with `verifier_score_coverage` 1.0. **Exp2 steps 2 and 4 are not fully scored:** 120 attempted / 100 scored, with 20 SWE-route attempts excluded on Daytona snapshot-quota infrastructure failures ("Maximum allowed: 45") before capacity cleared — these are infrastructure exclusions, not task failures.
- **Verifier mean (`eval/all/avg_verifier_score`)** = the mean over the scored validation attempts of **bounded normalized verifier scores** [P: pinned eval code https://github.com/marin-community/marin/blob/0c1e81ca93635ac157db90ff786a8f8fba65b13c/vendor/skyrl/skyrl-train/skyrl_train/evaluate.py (computes the metric from `normalized_verifier_scores`) and https://github.com/marin-community/marin/blob/0c1e81ca93635ac157db90ff786a8f8fba65b13c/vendor/skyrl/skyrl-gym/skyrl_gym/verification.py#L127 (`normalized_verifier_score` scales each verified score's declared native bounds to [0,1])]. "Raw" in this ledger means **unshaped/unpenalized** (no stop/truncation/shaping adjustments), **not** the native unscaled verifier reward. The per-route declared score bounds and any consumed-config normalization parameters are [M] (not published here). [R: `recovery13-eval18-best18-step19-milestone-20261115.json`, `recovery13-rl-compute-complete-best20-milestone-20261115.json`]
- **`avg_score` ("penalized mean")** = `eval/all/avg_score`: reported alongside the verifier mean in the same receipt and differs from it through stop-related adjustments (the receipts record the components: `completed_stop_score_contribution`, `length_stop_score_contribution`, `truncation_penalized`, `rollout_retries`). It is the mean of the optimization-reward channel; the receipts name these component fields but do not restate the full numerical relation between `avg_score` and the normalized verifier mean. STATE.md calls this the "penalized mean" for step 20 (0.2659166666666667 vs verifier mean 0.26758333333333334) [R: STATE.md L51].
- **pass@1** = `eval/all/pass_at_1`: the **fraction of distinct validation task identities (UIDs) with at least one passing trajectory**, per the pinned implementation — the batch's trajectories are grouped by UID, each UID passes if *any* of its trajectories passes, and `pass_at_n = (passing UIDs) / (distinct UIDs in the batch)` [P: https://github.com/marin-community/marin/blob/0c1e81ca93635ac157db90ff786a8f8fba65b13c/vendor/skyrl/skyrl-train/skyrl_train/trajectory_runners/trajectory_processing.py#L94-L98]. An attempt passes when its verification result carries an explicit pass verdict; when no verdict is supplied it falls back to a positive outcome reward (> 0); masked/non-VERIFIED outcomes fail [P: same file, `get_trajectory_passes` #L159-L170 — definition verified against the local marin checkout at the pinned revision]. It is **not** "the fraction with verifier reward exactly 1.0" (an earlier wording here was wrong). **Denominator (round-10 correction):** the denominator is the batch's **distinct UIDs (attempted identities), not "the 120 scored validation attempts"** — an excluded/masked attempt **fails rather than leaving the denominator** (`get_trajectory_passes` marks excluded rows as failures). With one attempt per UID in these validations, a complete validation's denominator is the 120 attempted identities; for the incomplete eval2/eval4 the raw pass channel's denominator would still be the 120 attempted identities while the normalized verifier mean is computed over the 100 scored attempts — the earlier "120 scored validation attempts" generalization is withdrawn (it holds only for the fully scored validations). The raw channel (`eval/all/raw_verifier_pass_at_1`) is computed by the pinned evaluator on a copy of the batch **without** `exclude_from_baseline` [P: https://github.com/marin-community/marin/blob/0c1e81ca93635ac157db90ff786a8f8fba65b13c/vendor/skyrl/skyrl-train/skyrl_train/evaluate.py#L233-L235], so the two channels can differ whenever exclusions exist; their equality in every receipt read here is an **observed result of these fully scored validations**, not a general identity. (e.g., exp1 step 16: 35/120 = 0.2916667 — 35 passing UIDs of the 120 attempted identities — which sits **above** step 16's verifier mean 0.2834167, a different aggregate (mean of bounded normalized scores over the scored attempts), not an exactly-1.0 count.)
- **Route scores:** per-validation-route aggregates keyed `eval/<route>` in the same receipts. **SWE** = `eval/long-loop-smartie-swesmith` (agentic SWE-smith validation route, 20 tasks), **Nemotron** = `eval/nvidia_Nemotron-RL-Ultra-Training-Blends` (non-agentic Nemotron-blend validation route, 80 tasks), **Structure** = `eval/structure-closure` (non-agentic structure-closure validation route, 20 tasks); 20 + 80 + 20 = the 120 validation attempts [R: `validation-step-16.json`; STATUS.md L866]. **The route values recorded here are route pass fractions — route pass@1 = route passes / route attempts — not route reward means.** They weight to the overall pass@1, not to the overall verifier mean. Step 16: 7/20, 21/80, 7/20 → (7 + 21 + 7)/120 = 35/120 = 0.2916667. Step 14: the STATUS.md receipt records the three fractions **0.35 / 0.30 / 0.30** [R: STATUS.md L834], i.e. SWE 7/20, Nemotron 24/80, **structure 6/20** (0.30 × 20 = 6), summing to 37/120 = 0.3083333 = the recorded overall pass@1; a table row elsewhere in this ledger previously wrote "structure 7/20 = 0.35", which is internally inconsistent (0.35 ≠ 0.30 and would total 38/120) — corrected 2026-11-15T17:42Z. The receipts record route **fractions** with denominators 20/80/20; no receipt states a different route denominator, and the recorded overall pass@1 (0.3083333) confirms the 20/80/20 split. **Raw route reward means are not in the receipts compiled here and are unavailable**; the overall mean is therefore not recomputable from the published route numbers.
- **Timestamp types:** *event time* = the trainer/eval finish timestamp recorded inside a milestone receipt (`evalNN_finished_utc` / eval-event `time`); *readback time* = when the observer later read the receipt or aggregate (`recorded_utc` / `observed_utc`, always later); *upper bound* = a logged observation time that only bounds the event from above (no precise finish timestamp exists). Each table timestamp below is labeled accordingly.

## Scheduled, fully scored, and window-eligible observations

Validations are **scheduled** every 2 optimizer updates (steps 2, 4, 6, …, 20 in exp2). Three distinct notions (round-9 correction, aligned to the pinned selection code):

- **Fully scored** (score eligibility): an observation whose 120 scheduled attempts all scored (0 excluded). Exp2 steps 2 and 4 are not fully scored (20 SWE-route attempts excluded on Daytona snapshot-quota infrastructure failures); steps 6–20 are fully scored.
- **Window-eligible** (checkpoint/window eligibility): the pinned code forms each candidate window from the **five consecutive scheduled steps at interval 2** (`step − 4·interval … step`) and returns **no value when any scheduled step in that exact range is absent from the history** [P: https://github.com/marin-community/marin/blob/0c1e81ca93635ac157db90ff786a8f8fba65b13c/vendor/skyrl/marinskyrl/checkpoint_paths.py#L33-L46 `trailing_validation_ema`]. A missing or incomplete **scheduled** observation therefore **breaks the window** — the code does not skip it and reach further back. (Round-8/earlier wording — "eligible observations only", "five consecutive eligible observations" — could be read as skipping incomplete scheduled steps; that reading is withdrawn. ~~Whether the exp2 trainer's consumed copy behaves identically to this pinned code is a separate round-2 dirty-build question, unverified.~~ **Round-10 update:** the round-2 public pin [`038ad7f188…`](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/blob/038ad7f188a889dfed07cac9a29fe34eabd7e5cd/vendor/skyrl/marinskyrl/checkpoint_paths.py#L33-L46)'s `trailing_validation_ema` is **textually identical** to the round-1 pin's function above (verified in the local marin checkout; the file blob differs between the two commits only in one import line, `marinskyrl.resource_locator` vs the relative `.resource_locator`). What remains unverified is only the **executed job's** consumed-config/launch binding (private receipts [M]) — the tracked selection implementation itself is recovered for both rounds; see build-provenance.md.)

Because exp2 steps 2 and 4 are the only incomplete scheduled observations and they fall before step 6, every candidate window from step 14 onward consists of five fully scored scheduled observations — so "five consecutive scheduled, fully scored observations" and any skip-based reading happen to give the same result after step 6. The first complete window is steps **6/8/10/12/14** [R: `recovery13-eval14-first-best-milestone-20261115.json` `eligible_steps`].

- **EMA recomputation recipe (round-9 correction; round-10 identities fixed; verified against the published series):** the published EMA values are the **normalized finite-window weighted mean over the five scheduled validations at interval 2**, not a recursive span-5 recurrence. With x_s = the verifier mean at scheduled step s, window = 5, interval = 2, **alpha = 2/(window+1) = 1/3 and decay = 1 − alpha = 2/3** (the pinned code computes `decay = 1 - 2/(window+1)`; the earlier line here wrote "α = 1 − 2/(window+1) = 1/3", which mislabels decay as alpha — that expression evaluates to 2/3 and is the *decay*, corrected 2026-11-15T18:45Z):
  `EMA_t = Σ_{j=0..4} (2/3)^j · x_(t−2j) / Σ_{j=0..4} (2/3)^j`
  — the same form as the pinned code `trailing_validation_ema` [P: https://github.com/marin-community/marin/blob/0c1e81ca93635ac157db90ff786a8f8fba65b13c/vendor/skyrl/marinskyrl/checkpoint_paths.py#L33-L46], which requires **all five scheduled steps** present (returns no value otherwise), weights `decay^age` with `age` 0 (newest) → 4 (oldest), and normalizes by the weight sum. The previously published recursive recipe `EMA_t = (1−α)·EMA_{t−1} + α·x_t` seeded at the first eligible observation is **withdrawn — it does not reproduce the table** (it yields 0.20075076301497646 / 0.22830941001496408 / 0.23284516223219826 / 0.24442455259924328 at steps 14/16/18/20).
  Tiny recomputation from the published verifier-mean column (Python ≥ 3; pairs the oldest
  reward with the SMALLEST weight (2/3)^4 and the newest with weight 1 — weights increase
  from oldest to newest, the same pairing as the pinned code, so the printed values match
  the published column to the last digit at steps 14/16/18; step 20 differs in the last
  digit from a literal re-implementation of the pinned code, see below):
  ```python
  x = {6: 0.20007916874930573, 8: 0.1920711965509095, 10: 0.17641340216121468,
       12: 0.1951813725490196, 14: 0.21824999999999997, 16: 0.2834267040149393,
       18: 0.24191666666666664, 20: 0.26758333333333334}
  for t in (14, 16, 18, 20):
      rewards = [x[t - 2*j] for j in range(4, -1, -1)]        # oldest first
      weights = [(2/3)**age for age in range(4, -1, -1)]      # (2/3)^4 ... 1
      print(t, sum(r*w for r, w in zip(rewards, weights)) / sum(weights))
      # 14 0.2008526161737512  16 0.23259077361923453
      # 18 0.238219238827311   20 0.25261617680247456
  ```
  This reproduces the published EMA column at steps 14 (0.2008526161737512), 16 (0.23259077361923453) and 18 (0.238219238827311), and at step 20 gives 0.25261617680247456 — the published/receipt value. **Last-digit qualification (round-10):** the pinned code computes its decay constant as `1 - 2/(window+1)` = 0.6666666666666667, while the snippet's literal `2/3` is 0.6666666666666666; substituting the code's constant into the same weighted-mean expression yields step-20 **0.2526161768024745** — one final-digit (≈6e−17) lower than the published value, with steps 14/16/18 identical either way. The published series therefore agrees with both forms to ~15 significant digits, and the step-20 difference is a floating-point artifact of the decay-constant representation/summation order, not an arithmetic error; it has no effect on the ranking (the nearest competing EMA value, step 18's 0.2382…, is ~0.014 away). "Digit-for-digit equality with the pinned implementation" (earlier wording here and in the notebook) is withdrawn. The recipe applies to exp2's published series; exp1 has no equivalent published series (see Unavailable).
