Hero checkpoint merge results

Merges of the hero's final checkpoints, scored in memory against the raw checkpoint. No merged weights were written. Windows and decision rules were fixed before results. No merge here reaches the -0.005 bar set for a merged starting point. Separately, the merged checkpoint posted on Marin #8435 is now being tested as the launch start in the overnight runs, and that decision is still open.

  • Merging the 65K tail helps general loss at 4K, 65K and 262K, and both main candidates raise MRCR recovery a little.
  • A merge across a context switch looks viable so far. One step across costs almost nothing on loss, and half weight on pre-switch weights keeps 99% of the recovery gain in one test.
  • Not tested: real extension checkpoints, which do not exist yet, or whether a gain survives SFT.

Uniform windows ending at raw step-390000, 6,000 steps apart, qk 1.66. Change in clean macro. Blend across the 262K switch

Linear blends of raw step-390251 and Keep 1.66 after 100 training steps at 262K, qk 1.66. Final verdict by the fixed rule: recovery is "robust" (micro and macro f(0.5) = 0.992), and "interior beats both ends on loss" is not met. Recovery saturates quickly while loss rises about linearly with w, so the 390351 endpoint trades loss for recovery. w = 0.5 keeps 99% of the recovery at about raw's loss on 262K; w = 0.25 keeps 72% and gains 0.00089 at 262K, level at 4K. It is one 100-step run at one-rack batch, so it says nothing final about extension checkpoints.

Raw clean macro: 4K 1.68745, 262K 1.64710. Guards pass at both ends with limit 0.0001 on loss and 0.001 on recovery. The w = 0 loss guard reads log lines at 3 decimals, so about 0.00007 of its differences can be rounding: treat it as a low-resolution pass.

Why qk matters here

Weights trained 100 steps at Global 2.00 lose retrieval at qk 1.66 (recovery 0.183), while weights trained at 1.66 keep it at 2.00. So a merge that spans a qk change may behave differently from the blend above, which had no qk change. This has not been tested.
