What would need to be true for each candidate

  • Keep 1.66: the other candidates' lower full NLL on hard cases would have to be noise or too small to matter. They are 0.0004 to 0.0013 lower at the farthest band and 0.0007 to 0.0020 at 8 needles, with every interval including zero.
  • Global 1.88: a full NLL change of -0.0003 [-0.0011, +0.0006] overall would have to be enough, and the 0.0015 [0.0003, 0.0028] higher full NLL on 2 needles at the farthest band would have to be acceptable. Early grad norm reaches 6.31.
  • Global 2.00: its hard-case changes would have to hold on another seed: recovery +0.0023 at the farthest band and +0.0031 at 8 needles, full NLL -0.0011 overall. A grad norm of 11.30 on the first step would have to stay harmless over a longer run.
  • Log 32768: full NLL of -0.0009 [-0.0019, +0.0000] overall and -0.0020 at 8 needles would have to hold on another seed, and serving support would have to land before export. Early grad norm reaches 5.37.

What is and isn't separated

  • Merged vs raw: clean macro is 0.0017 lower on merged at each length, against about 0.003 before training, and raw is higher on all 23 subsets at 262K. Recovery and full NLL do not separate them, overall or in the hard cells.
  • Clean macro: does not separate merged recipes. Every arm is within 0.0004 of Keep 1.66 at each length, inside the jitter band.
  • Overall recovery: saturated at 0.982 to 0.984. Per-arm intervals overlap, though two paired comparisons exclude zero, and log arms aren't like-for-like (see below).
  • Hard cases: some paired changes exclude zero, mainly Global 2.00 on recovery. All are small, uncorrected, and from one seed.
  • Train CE: Global 1.88, Global 2.00 and Log 32768 stay below Keep 1.66 by 3.0e-4 to 4.4e-4 over the last 20 steps, beyond the 1.5e-4 one-rack band. The gaps are shrinking, so 100 steps do not show where they settle.
  • All NLL and recovery intervals: they resample MRCR sources only. No arm has a repeat, so training noise is not in them.

Held-out loss, from two kinds of eval

Kept apart on purpose. The in-loop eval runs Paloma only and has no Uncheatable, so it gives 12 of the clean 19 subsets. It also drops each subset's final partial window, where offline pads it: ptb and wikitext_103 have no in-loop window at all. On the same final weights, in-loop reads 0.0040 above offline for the same 12 subsets, in all seven arms (0.00401 to 0.00405). So differences between arms carry over, and levels do not. Offline points are not joined: nothing was measured between them. Bands sit around Keep 1.66 at each step: inner 0.0002, outer 0.0007, measured offline on the clean 19, and not measured for the in-loop subset.

MRCR: two measured points only

Step 390,251 is each arm's no-training baseline and step 390,351 its final eval, not joined. No MRCR eval ran in between, and nothing is interpolated. Keep 1.66 starts at recovery 0.301 and full NLL 0.786; the path between is not measured.

Questions

  • Is the merged start safe? It pairs averaged weights with optimizer state and bias terms from the last step, which has not been tried before. Merged and raw Keep 1.66 share seed, data and code, so early train CE and grad norm compare directly.
  • Does the merge gain survive 100 steps? The merge starts about 0.003 ahead on clean macro.
  • Which recipe, at the launch batch? Constant scaling is bracketed at 1.66, 1.88 and 2.00, beside three log forms.

Common settings

  • 11 racks, 704 GPUs, CP4 x EP16, batch 176, 262,144 tokens, capacity factor 1.5, seed 0.
  • Data: the long-context mix at 4x (43.9% long tokens).
  • 100 steps, to step 390351, on the extension schedule: 5% to 1% of peak over 4,335 steps.
  • In-loop evals every 40 steps, then a final eval after the final save. Includes the eval memory fix, #10994.
  • After each arm: MRCR and held-out at 4K, 65K and 262K, compared with that recipe's no-training baseline on the same start.

What is and isn't resolved

  • Resolved: no arm failed. Drops (mean 2.9e-4, max 5.8e-4 to 6.0e-4) and step time (29.2 to 29.5 s) match across all seven.
  • Not separated: clean macro across the six merged arms. The spread is 0.0003 at 4K (1.6866 to 1.6869), 0.0004 at 65K (1.6475 to 1.6479) and 0.0004 at 262K (1.6451 to 1.6455), all within checkpoint jitter of 0.0002 to 0.0007.
  • Recovery: all seven are 0.982 to 0.984 with overlapping per-arm intervals. Overlap is not a paired test: paired, Log 65536 squared minus Keep 1.66 is +0.0013 [+0.0002, +0.0024] and Log 32768 minus Global 2.00 is -0.0014 [-0.0023, -0.0005]. Log arms' recovery is not like-for-like, because their rows run at different qk.
  • Partly separated: full and distractor NLL. Full NLL is 0.0249 to 0.0265 and distractor NLL 1.0648 to 1.0673 across merged arms. Some paired intervals exclude zero, as the table shows, but they resample MRCR sources only.
  • Not resolved: whether those NLL gaps exceed run-to-run training noise. There is no repeat of any arm at 11 racks, and each arm is one seed.
  • Not resolved: what the early grad norm spikes mean. The five arms with raised qk reach 5.37 to 19.65 at steps 390251 to 390252; Keep 1.66 on either start stays under 2.5 for all 100 steps. Mean train CE over 100 steps stays within 0.0006 across the merged arms. 100 steps do not show whether the spikes matter later.
  • Not comparable directly: the change from baseline. It runs from -0.0055 (Global 2.00) to +0.0024 at nearly the same final clean macro, because the baselines differ by recipe. Compare final values.
  • Not resolved: the effect of queue 64. Only one recipe ran at queue 64, so queue and recipe can't be separated here. Its logged peak memory is 130.77 GiB, against 132.52 GiB for the others.

How to read them

  • Clean macro leaves out ptb, wikitext_103, gab and twitterAAE. Checkpoint jitter is 0.0002 to 0.0007.

  • Each arm is one seed. Intervals resample MRCR sources, not training noise.
  • MRCR recovery is near its ceiling after training, and it is a copying task, so full-context and distractor NLL carry more of the comparison.
