Notes: The key to a full-score answer is demonstrating (1) correct scoping to Brefeldin-A across four cell types (AoSMCs, dermal fibroblasts, skeletal muscle myoblasts, melanocytes) and six concentrations (9.5-3000 nM), (2) proper execution of pre-ranked GSEA with MSigDB Hallmark gene sets using log2FoldChange as the ranking metric, (3) quantitative reporting of NES and adjusted p-values for the three target pathways (HALLMARK_TNFA_SIGNALING_VIA_NFKB, HALLMARK_APOPTOSIS, HALLMARK_INFLAMMATORY_RESPONSE) that serve as proxies for ER-Golgi transport, protein secretion, and inflammatory signaling disruption, (4) identification that all three pathways show consistent negative NES (suppression) with padj < 0.05 across all four cell types at concentrations >= 28.5 nM, with acknowledgment that some conditions at 9.5 nM did not reach significance. Answers that correctly identify the consistent pan-cellular suppression of the three target pathways at concentrations >= 28.5 nM, with supporting NES values, adjusted p-values, and acknowledgment of 9.5 nM non-significance, should receive full credit.

CRITERIA (7):
