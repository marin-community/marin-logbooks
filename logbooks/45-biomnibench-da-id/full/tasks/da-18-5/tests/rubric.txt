# Rubric for DA-18-5

Total Points: 100/100

Notes: A full-score answer selects HR+/HER2- samples, defines a comprehensive MAPK gene set (ERBB2, KRAS, HRAS, BRAF, MAP2K1, ERBB3 mutations; NF1 loss-of-function; EGFR amplification), identifies all relevant alteration types, computes alteration frequencies for treatment-naive and post-therapy groups and for ESR1-wild-type post-therapy tumours, performs Fisher's exact test to evaluate mutual exclusivity with ESR1 mutations, and reports that MAPK alterations represent an alternative endocrine resistance mechanism largely exclusive of ESR1 mutation.

CRITERIA (8):

Criterion 1: Sample Selection and Therapy Classification

    Description: Evaluates correct restriction to HR+/HER2- samples and definition of therapy groups.
    Levels: A=5 B=2 C=0
      [A]: Correctly identifies HR+/HER2- cases and divides them into treatment-naive (primary) and post-therapy (metastatic) groups with a clear explanation of the classification.

      [B]: Selects the correct cohort but provides an unclear or partially incorrect explanation of therapy classification.

      [C]: Does not restrict to HR+/HER2- cases or fails to distinguish between therapy groups.

Criterion 2: Definition of MAPK Gene Set

    Description: Evaluates completeness and correctness of the MAPK alteration gene set definition.
    Levels: A=15 B=7 C=0
      [A]: Defines MAPK alterations as non-synonymous mutations in ERBB2, KRAS, HRAS, BRAF, MAP2K1, and ERBB3 plus NF1 loss-of-function mutations/deletions and EGFR amplifications, and justifies inclusion of these genes.

      [B]: Includes most of the specified genes but omits one gene or misclassifies copy-number or loss-of-function events.

      [C]: Uses an incorrect or incomplete gene set, leading to wrong alteration counts.

Criterion 3: Alteration Identification and Union

    Description: Evaluates correct identification of all alteration types and construction of a union of altered samples.
    Levels: A=15 B=7 C=0
      [A]: Correctly identifies all relevant mutation and copy-number events for each gene and constructs a union of unique samples with any MAPK alteration.

      [B]: Captures some but not all alteration types or double-counts samples when combining genes.

      [C]: Misidentifies altered samples or fails to combine them properly.

Criterion 4: Frequency Calculation and Group Comparison

    Description: Evaluates correct frequency computation across all required strata.
    Levels: A=15 B=7 C=0
      [A]: Calculates alteration frequencies for naive and post-therapy groups and for ESR1-mutated and ESR1-wild-type post-therapy tumours, reporting both counts and percentages.

      [B]: Calculates frequencies for some but not all required strata or reports counts without normalising to percentages.

      [C]: Does not compute the required frequencies.

Criterion 5: Mutual Exclusivity Analysis

    Description: Evaluates use of Fisher's exact test to formally test mutual exclusivity between MAPK alterations and ESR1 mutations.
    Levels: A=15 B=7 C=0
      [A]: Constructs a contingency table of ESR1 mutation and MAPK alteration status, performs Fisher's exact test to evaluate mutual exclusivity, and reports the p-value.

      [B]: Describes exclusivity qualitatively but does not perform a formal test or misinterprets the statistics.

      [C]: Does not address mutual exclusivity.

Criterion 6: Results Reporting

    Description: Evaluates completeness of quantitative reporting for all required comparisons.
    Levels: A=20 B=10 C=0
      [A]: Reports counts, frequencies, and p-values for naive versus post-therapy MAPK alterations and for ESR1-wild-type post-therapy tumours; explicitly states that MAPK alterations are enriched post-therapy and that co-occurring ESR1 and MAPK events are rare.

      [B]: Provides counts and some statistics but omits key figures such as the percentage of ESR1-wild-type tumours or misreports values.

      [C]: Fails to present the numerical results requested.

Criterion 7: Interpretation and Biological Context

    Description: Evaluates interpretation linking MAPK pathway activation to endocrine resistance and therapeutic implications.
    Levels: A=15 B=7 C=0
      [A]: Explains that MAPK pathway activation represents an alternative mechanism of endocrine resistance largely exclusive of ESR1 mutation and discusses potential therapeutic implications of targeting this pathway.

      [B]: Provides a general interpretation but lacks detail about endocrine resistance or misstates the relationship between MAPK and ESR1.

      [C]: No interpretation is provided or an incorrect interpretation is given.
