* Summary
- *Goal.* Snowball v0.1 says it is ChatGPT/OpenAI (thinking on) or Nemotron/NVIDIA (thinking off).
  We want the model itself, with no system prompt, to know it is Marin's Latest MoE, developed and
  trained by the Marin Community, without losing capability.
- *Method.* Short SFTs from v0.1 (18, 36 or 72 updates) on Will Held's identity dataset
  (marin-community/identity-data @665ac6e) mixed with replay of v0.1's own answers.
- *Main finding.* Identity rows rendered with thinking ON teach the model to "answer and stop"
  in thinking mode. BFCL tool calling falls from 27.6% to about 15%. This happens whether the
  thinking span is empty or holds GLM-written reasoning. Identity rows rendered with thinking OFF do not do this.
- *Lead candidate: A13.* It combines:
  - identity rows rendered with thinking OFF;
  - a small share (179 rows, 10%) of thinking-ON identity rows with GLM reasoning;
  - the thinking-ON tool-calling replay rows, doubled.

  Results:
  - BFCL 28.3%, +0.7 points [-3.4, +4.4] vs v0.1;
  - with thinking ON, it never claims another lab (v0.1: 46 of 52);
  - GSM8K -1.1 [-1.9, -0.3]; MATH500 unchanged.
- *Fallback: A3.* It renders identity rows with thinking OFF only.
  - Best BFCL: 31.1%.
  - But its reasoning often invents a "system message" (17 of 52).
  - GSM8K -1.7 [-2.6, -0.9], which trips our pre-set line.
- *What did not help:*
  - more training (72 updates);
  - less training (18 updates: BFCL collapses);
  - a tiny Tulu-style set;
  - linear merges of A3 and A8a;
  - self-distillation from v0.1 for thinking-ON rows (its reasoning always quotes the system prompt).

* Evaluation setup
- *BFCL parity.* 123 tool-calling tasks run with the Pi 0.87.0 agent. Each model gets five full runs,
  with a run-level bootstrap for 95% CIs against a fresh five-run v0.1 baseline. Pi sends no
  temperature, so the server default (1.0) applies. That is why single runs are noisy (one checkpoint swung 29 to 17).
- *Identity.* 52 core identity prompts plus 14 third-person controls, thinking off and on, no
  system prompt. Answers are read with special tokens kept, so reasoning and answer split at the
  model's own end-of-thinking token. Only 31 of the 52 core prompts ask for the name. The rest ask
  about the creator, where naming the creator alone is correct.
- *Maths.* Paired GSM8K (thinking off) and MATH500 (thinking on), 3 decodes each, item-level
  bootstrap. Pre-set stop line: a drop of more than 1.5 points with the whole CI below zero.
- *SWE100.* 100 SWE tasks with mini-swe-agent 2.1. Only runs with all 100 tasks scored count.

* What we learned
- *The thinking mode is where tool calling breaks.* The Pi agent runs with thinking on. If most
  thinking-on training examples are short identity answers with no tool call, the model learns to
  answer and stop. Keeping thinking-on identity rows to about 10%, and doubling thinking-on tool
  replay, avoids it.
- *Training length matters non-monotonically.* At 18 updates BFCL is collapsed, and by 36 it has
  recovered. Checking one checkpoint can mislead; future runs should check several.
- *The GSM8K cost (1--2 points) comes with 36-update training in every recipe.* It is not the
  identity data alone. A better replay mix is the likely fix (untested).
- *Merging does not separate the two strengths.* BFCL and the reasoning quirk switch together near
  t = 0.5.
- *Parser artefact.* Our first "thinking-on format broken" counts came from a serve that did not
  split reasoning from the answer. They hit v0.1 just as hard (52 of 52). Reading with special tokens
  kept removes the artefact.
- *Measurement.* BFCL at temperature 1.0 needs five or more runs per model. Single runs swing by
  10 points.
